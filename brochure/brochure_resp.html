About the Programme

Transforming Healthcare Through Prevention

A Legacy of Excellence in Preventive Medicine

Preventive Medicine focuses on the health of individuals and communities in order to promote, protect and maintain health and well-being and to prevent disease, disability and premature death.

The Programme equips residents with a sound and broad foundation in Public Health and/or Occupational Medicine, with the knowledge, skills and experiences to become future leaders in health and healthcare systems.

A/Prof Jason Yap

Programme Director

"With the challenges facing Singapore, Preventive Medicine physicians are needed as never before, but they must prove their value to the organisations and the society they serve, and justify the leadership positions that they are placed in."

— SMA News, 2018 [Source]

Mission-Driven

Programme Structure

Your Journey to Specialist Accreditation

A comprehensive 5-year training pathway.

Junior Residency

(Year 1-3)

  • Master of Public Health degree
  • Core rotations in primary care, infectious disease, and policy
  • Foundation competencies in epidemiology and biostatistics
  • Tailored rotations based on interests

Senior Residency

(Year 4-5)

  • Advanced rotations at 2+ sites
  • Leadership development
  • Research and publication
  • Exit examination preparation
