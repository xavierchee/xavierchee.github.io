Scholarship and Financial Aids Eligibility

Our scholarships and financial aids are ultra-competitive and awarded based on academic excellence and demonstrated potential. Successful candidates typically demonstrate:

  • Exceptional academic achievement in their undergraduate studies (typically top of their cohort with CGPA > 3.6 on a 4.0 scale, CAP score > 4.5 on a 5.0 scale or equivalent performance on other grading systems)
  • Recognition of excellence through awards, honours, or peer-reviewed publications
  • Academic distinction such as graduating at or near the top of their undergraduate cohort

How to Apply

Eligible applicants are considered for available scholarships when they submit their programme application by the deadline. No separate scholarship application is required.

Candidates seeking financial aids must submit reference letters directly through the Graduate Admission System. Requirements include:

  • Designate at least one referee who will receive automated email instructions for submission
  • Recommended referees include university faculty members or direct work supervisors
