Uniqueness of Our Programme

Why choose between coursework or research MSc when you can master both? Our MSc in Precision Medicine brings together world-class academics, clinicians, and industry pioneers to deliver a truly one-of-a-kind programme. With its multidisciplinary curriculum and distinctive capstone—offering pathways into research or industry—you’ll graduate ready to lead the future of healthcare.

  • Hybrid by Design

    Not just coursework. Not just research. A powerful blend of both.

  • Multi-discplinary at the Core

    From biomedicine to genomics to data science to AI, ethics to regulation to sustainable business to clinical care, learn across disciplines.

  • Expert-led, Real-world Focused

    Taught by distinguished experts at NUS Medicine and other NUS faculties, together with leaders from world-class research centres, hospitals, and precision medicine industries.

  • Capstone with Choice

    Academic research or industry internship—the path that fits your ambition.

Education That Fits Your Life: Flexible Study Pathways

Our programme recognizes that students come with diverse backgrounds, career goals, and life commitments. Choose from our customizable study routes:

A one-year programme (two-years if taken part-time) for preparing medical doctors, healthcare professionals, communicators, educators and policy makers seeking expertise in precision health and medicine.

A 2-year programme offering advanced training in precision health and medicine with a hands-on capstone project. Designed for those pursuing research and expertise in the field.

Flexible pathway with stackable courses suitable for working professionals seeking flexibility.

Credit Conversion Opportunity

If you enrol in the MScPHM programme, your EC credits can count toward your degree requirements (minimum CAP of 3.5 required).

Starting your MScPHM journey

You must enrol in the MScPHM programme within the 5-year validity period for your EC credits to be counted toward your degree.

Credit Validity Period

EC and GC credits are valid for credit conversion for up to 5 years from completion date.
