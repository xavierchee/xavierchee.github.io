Credit Conversion Opportunity

When enrolling in the MScPHM programme, your EC credits can count toward degree requirements (minimum CAP of 3.5/5.0 required).

Starting your MScPHM journey

You must enrol in the MScPHM programme within the 5-year validity period for your EC credits to be counted toward your degree.

Credit Validity Period

EC and GC credits are valid for credit conversion for up to 5 years from completion date.
